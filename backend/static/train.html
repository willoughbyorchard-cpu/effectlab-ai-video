YOUR FOOTAGE · YOUR MODEL · LOCAL TRAINING

Teach your own AI.
On your laptop.

Choose a folder or ZIP of matched training examples and press one button. EffectLab uploads the files to your own laptop, checks them, and automatically starts training if they pass the audit. No third-party AI renderer, pretrained generator, or NVIDIA GPU is required for the compact CPU configuration.

Training data must contain a target. Each example needs a reference photo, the original driving video, and the matching target video with the replacement already shown. Random unrelated videos alone cannot teach this model which person to replace. Once these files are uploaded and pass the audit, training starts automatically. This compact model starts from random weights, so training may still fail to learn a useful swap.
01

Select your training set

Choose a folder or ZIP; no chat uploads needed.

For individual files, keep the sequence folders when possible. Flat filenames should follow the pattern sequence_0001_reference.jpg, sequence_0001_driving.mp4 and sequence_0001_target.mp4. Dataset upload limit: 2 GB per upload.
Choose files first. Data will be sent only to the EffectLab engine URL below.
02

Connect your local engine

Run the engine on the same computer that stores your data.

The local engine must be running first. For best compatibility, open this page at http://127.0.0.1:7860/train.html.
The GitHub Pages version is only a website. It cannot train on GitHub's servers. This page talks to the local Python engine on your machine, which stores the training files and checkpoint on your disk.
03

Automatic training progress

Training begins automatically after upload and data checks pass.

Choose your folder or ZIP and press Upload & automatically train AI. You do not need to start training separately.


This compact experiment uses 64 × 96 pixel frames, four frames per clip and eight base channels. It writes a checkpoint locally. It may still be slow on a CPU and the resulting model may not perform a useful swap. Keep the laptop plugged in and stop if it becomes too hot.
